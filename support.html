Frequently Asked Questions

What is Travory?
Travory is a travel planning and journaling app that helps you document and organize your travel experiences.
How do I delete my account?
You can delete your account by going to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Please note this action cannot be undone.
How is my data protected?
We take your privacy seriously. Please review our Privacy Policy for detailed information about how we collect, use, and protect your data.
The app isn't working properly. What should I do?
First, try restarting the app. If the issue persists, try updating to the latest version. If you're still experiencing problems, please contact us with details about the issue.
How do I provide feedback?
We welcome feedback! You can email us at taismryotasis@gmail.com or submit a feature request through our GitHub repository.
Which platforms does Travory support?
Travory is currently available for iOS devices. We're working on bringing the app to more platforms in the future.

When Contacting Support

To help us assist you more efficiently, please include:

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The version of Travory you're using
  • A detailed description of your issue
  • Screenshots (if applicable)
  • Steps to reproduce the problem
